Introductory Psychology

Psychology 2e Study Guide

Concise, unit-by-unit study guides for Psychology 2e by Rose M. Spielman, William J. Jenkins and Marilyn D. Lovett (OpenStax, 2020).

The textbook is written for a one-semester introduction to psychology course. It grounds core concepts in both classic studies and current research, and its coverage of psychological disorders follows the DSM-5-TR. Each unit below matches one chapter of the book. Open a unit’s guide for condensed notes, key terms, numbered figures and tables, and a review quiz, or jump to the original chapter on OpenStax.

Book at a glance

The sixteen chapters differ a lot in length. Chapter 15, Psychological Disorders, is the longest with eleven sections, followed by Chapter 11, Personality, with nine. Seven chapters have four sections each. Use Figure 1 to budget study time, and select a bar to jump to that unit.

How each guide works

Every unit is a single page that follows the chapter’s own section order and is laid out the same way, so you always know where to look.

Each chapter of the textbook ends with the same five review pages. The unit guides link to them so you can test yourself after reading.

A numbered part of a chapter, such as 3.2 Cells of the Nervous System.

The note button at the bottom right of each unit page; it shows shortened key terms for the section you are reading.

End-of-chapter multiple-choice items for checking recall.

End-of-chapter questions that ask you to apply and evaluate ideas.

Reflection prompts that connect a topic to your own experience.
DSM-5-TR
Text revision of the fifth edition of the American Psychiatric Association’s Diagnostic and Statistical Manual of Mental Disorders; the book’s reference for classifying disorders.
