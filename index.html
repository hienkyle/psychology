    Introductory Psychology

    Psychology 2e Study Guide

    Concise, unit-by-unit study guides for Psychology 2e by Rose M. Spielman, William J. Jenkins, and Marilyn D. Lovett (OpenStax, 2020). The textbook is written for a one-semester introduction to psychology course. It grounds core concepts in both classic studies and current research, and its coverage of psychological disorders follows the DSM-5-TR.

    Each unit below matches one chapter of the book. Open a unit's guide for condensed notes, key terms, and numbered figures and tables, or jump to the original chapter on OpenStax.

    Book at a glance

    The sixteen chapters differ a lot in length. Chapter 15, Psychological Disorders, is the longest with eleven sections, followed by Chapter 11, Personality, with nine. Seven chapters have four sections each. Use Figure 1 to budget study time, and select a bar to jump to that unit.

    How each guide works

    Every unit page follows the chapter's own section order and is laid out the same way, so you always know where to look.

    Each chapter of the textbook ends with the same five review pages. The unit guides link to them so you can test yourself after reading.

    Table 1. End-of-chapter materials in Psychology 2e
    MaterialWhat it offersAnswers
    Key TermsDefinitions of the chapter's bolded terms—
    SummaryA recap of each section—
    Review QuestionsMultiple-choice checks of the contentSelected answers in the Student Solution Guide
    Critical Thinking QuestionsOpen questions that apply the conceptsSelected answers in the Student Solution Guide
    Personal Application QuestionsPrompts to relate topics to your own lifeNot provided, since answers vary by student

    Inside the chapters, the textbook also uses recurring feature boxes. The guides point these out where they add to a topic.

    Key terms — using this guide
    Unit
    One chapter of the textbook and its matching study-guide page, unit{n}.html.
    Section
    A numbered part of a chapter, such as 3.2 Cells of the Nervous System.
    Key-terms note
    The box on each unit page that condenses the chapter's Key Terms list.
    Review Questions
    End-of-chapter multiple-choice items for checking recall.
    Critical Thinking Questions
    End-of-chapter questions that ask you to apply and evaluate ideas.
    Personal Application Questions
    Reflection prompts that connect a topic to your own experience.
    DSM-5-TR
    The text revision of the fifth edition of the American Psychiatric Association's Diagnostic and Statistical Manual of Mental Disorders; the book's reference for classifying disorders.

    Based on the Psychology 2e preface.
